What is solar powered water purification?

Solar powered water purification is a way of cleaning dirty water using energy from the sun. Solar panels make electricity to run pumps and filters, or the heat and UV light of the sun is used directly to kill harmful germs. It does not need fuel or a big power grid, so it can work almost anywhere the sun shines.

Why does it matter?

Safe drinking water

Many people still drink water with germs in it. Purifying it helps stop diseases like diarrhoea and cholera.

Free energy source

Sunlight costs nothing, so there is no need to buy fuel or electricity to clean the water.

Works far from cities

Villages without a power line can still make clean water using the sun.

Good for nature

No smoke or fuel burning is needed, so there is less pollution in the air.

How does the sun clean the water?

Step 1: Collect

Dirty water from a well, river or pond is collected in a tank.

Step 2: Power up

Solar panels catch sunlight and turn it into electricity for the pump and cleaning parts.

Step 3: Filter and treat

The water passes through filters to remove dirt, then UV light or heat kills germs.

Step 4: Drink

Clean water is stored in a safe container and used for drinking and cooking.

Benefits of solar water purification

Lower costs

After the system is set up, running it costs very little because the sun is free.

Easy to maintain

Simple systems have few moving parts, so they are easy to look after.

Helps remote places

Small units can serve schools, clinics and homes that are far from the main grid.

Healthier communities

Clean water means fewer sick days and more time for study and work.
